Person A vs Person B comparisons, where Person B buys at about the same price or less than per week than Person A.

One caveat: If the divisor of the large product by the small product is a factor of X.75 or larger, (where X is an integer) 
we round up. For example, if the larger item is $7.29 and the smaller is $2.49, the factor is 2.93, so we will buy 3 of the small product instead of 2. Similarly, if a product is $4.00, and the smaller size is $2.25, we will buy 2 of the smaller size, even if it is larger. Similarly, if a product is X.745, we this will round up to X.75.
